The BRIEF Method

A memorable framework that helps you give AI the information it needs to produce a useful answer.

B

Background

Give AI the context it needs.

R

Role

Tell AI who it should act as.

I

Instruction

State exactly what you need done.

E

Examples

Show the style, format or direction.

F

Format

Define how the final answer should look.
